Your phone can't come. Your people can.

214 of 415 athletes who say online why they carry a phone say: just in case.

Shorelist is for that case.

Source: 2,100 public Reddit comments about sport without a phone, collected on 18 and 19 October 2026 and coded by our team. 415 gave a reason for carrying one; 214 said in case something goes wrong, which we shorten to just in case. They are mostly runners and riders. Of the 47 who do a water sport, none gave that reason; their first one was being reachable (38 %).

surf: between sets

For the surfer who is on call for the family. Today a call means paddling in and walking up to the car while the set he waited for breaks behind him. With shorelist on, NOOR lights on his wetsuit collar, in the board's shadow. One press, a few words through the short cable to his ear, and he paddles back out.

swim: the far buoy

For the swimmer whose phone waits on the towel, lighting up for everyone while she can answer no one. With shorelist on, the shell sits on her goggle strap and only TEO's call comes through, as a ring in her swim earbud. She treads water, presses once, talks, and swims on.

run: kilometre nine

For the runner whose phone fights him from a pocket it barely fits. With shorelist on, the phone stays face down on the hall table and the shell rides on his wrist. JO calls; he slows, presses once, talks, and picks the pace back up.
