Nine in ten train with no phone

Forty-two millimetres. About as thin as a watch.

The whole top is the button. Press it, hold it, rotate it: a click you can feel with gloves on, sealed with no moving part through the shell. The top is a dome with a scalloped rim and ribs that fan across it like a shell, so a wet finger finds it without looking.

Two lines of text under a smoked top: the caller's name, the next track, your pace. It lights when there is something to say and disappears when there is not.

Two hooks take any 20 mm strap: your watch strap, a collar, a goggle strap, a bag. Ø42 mm, about 10.2 mm at the top of the dome and under 30 g: targets, not yet measured. Five flush magnetic pads on the side take a wired headset and the charger.

Questions we were asked

Watch Why not a cellular Apple Watch?

A cellular watch costs like a phone (SE 3 cellular €329, Ultra 3 €899), does more than you need, ties you to one phone brand and fails wet: the touchscreen stops working and the microphone floods. Seashell works in salt water by clicks, costs €199 for the hub, and works with any phone and, on land, any Bluetooth earbuds.

Water Does it work under water?

Radio does not travel through water, for any device. Seashell is worn above the waterline, on a collar, a cap, a goggle strap or the wrist, and reconnects when it surfaces. In the water the sound goes through a waterproof wired headset on the magnetic pads. We test the link at sea in January 2027 and publish what we find.

Your gear I already have a Garmin and AirPods.

Keep them. Seashell adds what they cannot do: answer and call wet, be reachable with the phone at home, sound in the water. Bluetooth to your earbuds on land, cable in the water, next to the watch you train with.

Quiet I train to disconnect. Will people disturb me?

Only the people you name get through. Seashell says who is calling and shows the name on two lines of text; there is no touchscreen and no feed to pull you in. Everyone else waits until you are back.

Buttons Voice only?

No. The whole top is a button you press without looking, with gloves, wet. Press it to answer, rotate it to skip, set the volume or type a PIN. Voice is there for when your hands are busy.

Price Why €199?

It replaces the phone in your sport and the cellular watch you would otherwise buy, for less than either. €199 is the hub alone, and nothing is paid before pre-order opens. The eSIM data plan is priced separately and published before pre-order. A waterproof headset for the water is not included.

Battery How long does it last?

About 5.5 hours of calls or about 10 hours of music per charge. That is our estimate for the 330 mAh cell chosen for the round body, calculated from the parts, not measured. We publish measured hours after the prototype tests.

When When can I buy it?

Pre-order opens in 2027, after the sea test and the supplier quotes. The waitlist takes an email only; nothing is charged.

Rating Is it certified?

Not yet. The design target is IPX8: 3 m for 2 h in salt water, plus a 96 h salt-spray test. CE marking comes before the first batch.
